Numbers & money
Malay prices carry no decimal point when spoken — the currency word is the point. Everything before ringgit or dolar is dollars, everything after is cents. Then the shortcuts start: sen gets dropped, and setengah stands in for fifty, so 5.50 arrives as lima ringgit lima puluh sen, lima lima puluh or lima setengah. Two things are worth drilling on their own — belas against puluh, and cents under ten, which keep their small number and can sound like the whole price.
